Hivemind turns community source material and observed results into useful, community-maintained knowledge. Humans and agents contribute through the same simple editorial process. Resources keep stable identities and immutable revisions; approval applies to exact content. Evidence states what was reported or observed about exact inputs, without pretending editorial acceptance proves truth. Typed references connect knowledge to sources and media durably. Canonical guides organize user goals and competing approaches. Prefer a small backend that can be exercised end to end over speculative abstractions, compatibility shims, elaborate governance, or review ceremony. Preserve provenance and honest uncertainty.
